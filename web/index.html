How StackSense decides

1 Validatetypes, ranges, enums, required fields; defaults recorded as assumptions
2 Profilescale tier, concurrency, req/s, data, effort vs capacity, region, description signals
3 Pre-flightcontradictory hard constraints → infeasible; impossible requirements relaxed with critical warnings
4 Filterhard constraints exclude candidates: residency, compliance, deployment model, cloud preference, lifecycle
5 Scoreweighted factors: priorities × KB scores, skills, learning curve, ops burden, scale fit, budget fit, requirement fit
6 Cost & re-plantiered cost model; if the floor exceeds budget, re-plan with cost weighted at 5
7 Explainjustification, driven_by, why-not alternatives, trade-offs, warnings, decision trace

Guarantees

  • Deterministic: no clock, randomness or network in the engine; ties break on maturity then id. Same input → byte-identical JSON (see input_hash).
  • LLM-free decisions: the stack is chosen only by rules and weighted scoring over a curated, cited knowledge base. The optional AI button only suggests form values; it is off by default (STACKSENSE_LLM=off).
  • Hard constraints are never violated silently: violating candidates are excluded with a reason that appears in the alternatives and decision trace.
  • Explainable: every point of every score is a recorded factor tied to input fields.
